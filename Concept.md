# The Idea

Studies have shown that spraying cold water on your face in the morning helps a person wake up, I however am often too tired to even reach for a spray bottle to spray my face. So why not automate it. The system will work as a sort of morning alarm that will wake the user up at their chosen time by spraying mist and sounding an alarm that is behind the mist forcing the user to go closer and through the water to turn off the alarm, which hopefully will wake them up.

## Functionality
The system should be able to...
- Spray water
- Allow user to refill water
- Sound alarm
- Allow user to stop alarm
- Keep track of current time and allow user to select time for the alarm to go off
